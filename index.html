FAQ

Common questions.

What areas of Auckland do you service?

We are based in Auckland. Tell us your suburb in the quote form and we will confirm whether we can help.

Do you offer weekly and fortnightly cleaning?

Yes. Regular cleaning can be weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Tell us what suits your household when you request a quote.

Do you bring your own cleaning equipment?

We will confirm equipment and products when we discuss your quote. If you would like us to use particular products, let us know.

Can I request a one-off deep clean?

Yes. Deep and move-out cleaning is available as a one-off service.

Do you provide Airbnb turnover cleaning?

Yes. We offer turnover cleaning for Airbnb and holiday rentals. Share your requirements in the quote form.

Can I request specific cleaning instructions?

Yes. Let us know in your quote request, or when we discuss your needs, and we will take note of what matters to you.

How do I receive a quote?

Fill in the quote form with a few details about your property. We review it, clarify anything we need, then send you a clear quote.

What happens if I need to change my booking?

Please contact us as early as you can and we will do our best to help. Specific change terms are confirmed when your booking is arranged.
