FAQ

Questions customers ask before booking.

Do you provide both one-off and regular services?

Yes. We provide one-off cleaning, move-in / move-out cleaning, regular home cleaning, commercial cleaning and ongoing property care.

Can you help with maintenance as well as cleaning?

Yes. We can help with small repairs, handyman tasks, deck and fence repairs, and general property maintenance.

Yes. We can bring our own equipment and cleaning products. If you have specific products you prefer, let us know.

Can I send photos for a quote?

Yes. Photos are very helpful and can make the quote faster and more accurate.

Do you service commercial properties?

Yes. We work with offices, clinics, retail shops, showrooms and small commercial spaces.

Which areas do you cover?

We mainly service Auckland and North Shore, including Albany, Browns Bay, Takapuna, Milford, Mairangi Bay, Rosedale, Birkenhead and Glenfield.
