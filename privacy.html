Privacy Policy

This page explains how Tree House Cleaning, part of Tree House Property Care in Auckland, New Zealand, handles the information you give us through this website.

What we collect

When you request a quote we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, property address and type, the service you need, bedrooms, bathrooms, cleaning frequency, preferred date and any message you add.

How we use it

We use these details to respond to your request, discuss your needs and provide a quote. We do not sell your information.

How it is sent

The quote form is delivered to our team by email through a third-party form service (FormSubmit). Your details therefore pass through that service on their way to our inbox.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing treehousenz9@gmail.com.
