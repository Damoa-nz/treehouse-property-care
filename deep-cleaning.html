FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between a regular and a deep clean?

A regular clean maintains a home that is already in good order. A deep clean goes further into kitchens, bathrooms and surfaces that need extra attention.

Is oven cleaning included?

Oven cleaning is available where requested. Please mention it in your quote request so we can include it.

Will a move-out clean meet my agent's requirements?

Requirements vary between landlords and agents. If you have a checklist, send it with your quote request and we will discuss it with you. We cannot promise a particular inspection outcome.
