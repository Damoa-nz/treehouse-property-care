FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What areas do you service?

We service Auckland, North Shore, Albany, Browns Bay, Takapuna, Milford, Mairangi Bay, Rosedale, Birkenhead, Glenfield and surrounding areas.

Do you provide regular cleaning?

Yes. We can provide weekly, fortnightly, monthly or custom cleaning schedules.

Do you provide commercial cleaning?

Yes. We clean offices, clinics, retail spaces, showrooms and other small commercial sites.

Can you do maintenance as well as cleaning?

Yes. Tree House Property Care is designed to help with cleaning, maintenance and outdoor care through one team.

Do you provide move-out cleaning?

Yes. We provide move-in and move-out cleaning. Photos and property details help us quote accurately.

Can I send photos for a quote?

Yes. Photos are very helpful. You can paste a photo link in the quote form or email us directly.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes, we can bring our own equipment and products. If you have specific products you prefer, let us know.

Are eco-friendly products available?

Yes, eco-friendly options are available on request.

How fast can I get a quote?

We usually respond as soon as possible once we receive the address, service type and job details.

Do you offer one-off jobs?

Yes. We provide both one-off and regular services.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes. We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, commercial owners, body corporates and real estate agents.

How do I book?

Use the quote form or call Sally/Jace directly. We’ll confirm scope, price and schedule before starting.
