FAQ

Common questions.

How often should I book a regular clean?

It depends on your household. Weekly suits busy homes, fortnightly is a balanced option and monthly works well as a regular reset. We will help you decide when we discuss your needs.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Please tell us your preference when you request a quote and we will confirm how access can be arranged.

Can I ask for particular areas to be prioritised?

Yes. Let us know what matters most in your quote request, and we will take note of it.
